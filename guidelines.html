Buying, Shipping, & Selling Guidelines

Buying Guidelines

  • Look for the green ✓ Ownership Verified badge. When a listing has this badge, the seller has already submitted a short video to CanvasCircle proving they physically possess that specific piece, and an admin reviewed and approved it. The badge confirms physical possession only, not authenticity, condition, or provenance — but it eliminates the most common scam pattern (someone listing a piece they don't actually have).
  • Even when the badge is present, ask the seller to send you their verification video directly (via email or Facebook Messenger). Review it yourself: confirm the handwritten card shows the seller's correct @handle, that the date is recent, that the artwork in the video matches the photos in the listing, and that the video looks authentic to you. The badge means an admin reviewed the same video — viewing it yourself is your own independent confirmation. Sellers are advised in the Selling Guidelines below to lead with that video, so you shouldn't have to push for it.
  • Feel free to request an additional, longer, more detailed video with today's date — there is no length cap when sellers send videos to you directly through email or Messenger. Ask the seller to pan slowly around the piece, show the back of the canvas / signature / edition number / any condition issues, and hold up a card with today's date. A serious seller will be happy to do this.
  • If a seller refuses or stalls when you ask for the verification video, do not proceed with the transaction and please report that member to the CanvasCircle team.
  • Check the seller's listed References on CanvasCircle first. If the seller has built up References, their seller page and listings show a "References" section with the display names and @handles of Established Members who have agreed to vouch for them. Each reference is a clickable @handle — open one of those vouching members' listings and reach out to them through their contact methods to ask how their transaction with the seller went. References are a two-party-consent system: anyone listed there has actively accepted being a reference for that seller, which makes this much stronger than a written testimonial the seller controls. If the seller has no on-platform references yet (or you want corroboration outside the platform), ask them directly whether they have past buyers off-platform you could contact.
  • Always use goods and services via PayPal or Venmo if it is a new seller with no listed References and no track record you can verify.

Selling Guidelines

Who can list: CanvasCircle is for collectors selling pieces from their own personal collections. The following are not permitted to list on this platform:
  • Artists selling their own original work — please use a platform built for artists.
  • Dealers, galleries, or gallery agents selling on behalf of artists or other clients.
  • Consigners — anyone listing art owned by someone else. The seller of record must be the legal owner of the piece they're offering.

Listings that fall into any of these categories will be removed and the account may be suspended.

✓ Get the Ownership Verified badge — free, fast, and it sells faster.

Submit a short video (≤7 sec) showing the artwork in your hands with a handwritten card displaying your @handle + today's date. The admin reviews; approved listings get a green ✓ badge that signals to buyers you actually have the piece. No fee.

How: Seller Portal → Edit listing → Submit verification video. Reviewed within a day or two.

Two things you must know:

  • Save a copy of your video locally. CanvasCircle deletes the video bytes ~24 hours after review for privacy. Keep your original on your phone so you can send it to interested buyers later (recommended — see below).
  • Editing artist, title, category, or primary image auto-revokes the badge. Your video shows the listing as it was when verified — change any of those four fields and the badge is automatically removed. Price, description, dimensions, condition, status, shipping edits are safe. Re-submit a fresh video after a content change to restore the badge.
Why every listing should be verified, how to use the video with buyers, what we keep + delete — full details →

Why verify every listing you can:

  • Eliminates the #1 buyer hesitation — "does this seller actually have the piece?" — before they even message you.
  • Faster path to a sale. Buyers are told to ask for the video when they reach out; having one ready means every conversation starts with that question already answered.
  • Verified listings can be filtered on the catalog. Signed-in browsers can show only Ownership Verified listings — being verified gets you into that filtered view.
  • The badge sticks with the listing. Verify once, benefit until it sells.
  • Your seller page shows a running count of verified listings — a public trust signal that compounds over time.

When a buyer reaches out — lead with the video. Buyers are instructed in our Buying Guidelines to ask you for the verification video so they can review it themselves. Your first message back to any interested buyer should attach (or link to, via your cloud storage) the short verification video you submitted to CanvasCircle. After that, continue normally — negotiate price, send paperwork (COA, appraisal), share additional photos, and if the buyer wants a longer, more detailed video with today's date, record one and send it through email or Facebook Messenger (no length cap when you send directly). This order keeps the buyer's biggest question answered first and shortens the path to a deal.

What we keep vs. delete: The video is stored privately — only the reviewing admin sees it via a short-lived signed URL. After review, the bytes are automatically deleted within ~24 hours. CanvasCircle retains only a cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint of the file (to detect reused videos across listings, a strong fraud signal) plus the basic audit metadata (status, timestamps, reviewer, optional rejection note).

Manual revocation by admin. CanvasCircle may also revoke a badge at any time for rule violations or buyer complaints (for example, if a buyer reports that you refused to send them the verification video when asked).

Full policy details: Terms of Use § 5 Ownership Verification and Privacy Policy.

Important: the badge confirms physical possession only, not authenticity, condition, or provenance. Submitting falsified, AI-generated, or reused videos will result in revocation, listing removal, and may result in your account being banned.

★ Build your References — the most powerful trust signal on the platform.

You can ask any Established Member to appear as a Reference on your seller profile — regardless of whether you're Established yet yourself. Accepted References show up by display name and @handle on every one of your listings and on your public seller page. Buyers are explicitly told in our Buying Guidelines to click through your References and reach out to those members to ask how their transactions with you went — references are the closest thing to a Yelp review the platform offers, and they matter even more for newer sellers without their own track record on CanvasCircle.

How: Seller Portal → Profile → Sales References → "Request a reference." Enter the @handle of an Established Member you've transacted with, send the request, wait for them to accept. You can also accept incoming reference requests from members who've bought from or sold to you (you must be an Established Member yourself to be requested).

Why pursue them, the rules, and how to use them well →

Why this matters more than you think:

  • The Ownership Verified badge answers "do you have the piece?" — References answer the bigger question: "are you someone I can transact with safely?"
  • References are two-party consent. Anyone listed as your reference has actively agreed to be vouching for you — that's much stronger than a written testimonial you control.
  • References compound. As you transact with more members and request/accept references in both directions, the connected web of vouches grows — and benefits everyone in it.
  • For high-value pieces, References are often what gets a buyer past the wire-transfer hesitation.

Rules: You can have up to 5 active outgoing reference requests (pending + accepted combined). There's no cap on how many sellers you may agree to be a reference for. The target of any reference request must be an Established Member — only Established Members can be vouched for. You don't have to be Established yourself to send a request, but you do have to be Established for someone else to request you as their reference. Coordinated mutual references between related accounts, or vouching for users you have no genuine experience with, can result in suspension or banning of all involved accounts.

Use them well: Only request references from members you've actually completed a transaction with. When a member accepts being your reference, treat that as a real commitment — they're putting their reputation alongside yours. If you ever find yourself in a dispute, your References may get pinged by the buyer, so transact honestly.

Full policy: Terms of Use § 5 (Sales References subsection).

Becoming an Established Member

There are two paths to Established Member status. Either one results in exactly the same badge and the same privileges.

Path 1 — Automatic promotion when you meet both criteria:

  • 4 or more active sale listings with the Ownership Verified badge
  • 3 or more accepted Sales References from other Established Members

Once you hit both at the same time, your account is promoted automatically — no application, no waiting period. You'll see a welcome banner on your dashboard the next time you sign in.

Path 2 — Direct admin promotion. CanvasCircle (the admin) can grant Established Member status directly to a user based on off-platform trust — for example, established collectors known personally to the admin who haven't yet built up the on-platform criteria. This path is especially common for the platform's initial seed cohort (early users who joined before there were enough Established Members in the network to vouch for them) and for collectors with strong reputations elsewhere in the art community. Direct admin promotion is at the admin's sole discretion and is the same status as an auto-promoted account — there is no second-class tier.

What being Established unlocks (either path):

  • Your listing edits go live without per-change admin review. Non-Established sellers' content edits re-queue the listing for admin moderation; Established sellers' edits go straight to the public catalog.
  • The 10-sale-listing cap is lifted. Non-Established Members can have at most 10 active sale listings at a time; Established Members have no limit. (ISO posts are uncapped for everyone.)
  • Other sellers can request you as a Sales Reference. (The reference request system requires the target to be Established — you can ask anyone to vouch for you, but they can only ask you to vouch for them if you're Established.)
  • Public Established Member badge visible on your seller page and listings to signed-in viewers.
  • Inclusion in the catalog's "Established Members" filter — signed-in browsers can show only listings from Established sellers.

Track your progress. Open your Seller Portal → Profile. If you're not yet Established, you'll see a "Path to Established Member" block showing your current count for each criterion. Sellers on Path 2 won't see this block once they've been promoted — the badge is already theirs.

CanvasCircle (the admin) also retains discretion to revoke Established status manually at any time for rule violations, deceptive conduct, or other behavior incompatible with the status — see our Terms of Use for the full policy.

Shipping Guidelines

  • Always obtain shipping insurance for the total purchase price.
  • For transactions $10,000 and less, UPS through Pirate Ship's website provides by far the best shipping rates and can fully insure the package for the sale price.
  • For transactions above $10,000, you will need to seek alternative shipping options to fully insure. We recommend utilizing goshippo.com to find the best shipping rates that will fully cover the transaction cost.
  • For packaging the art, Park West art boxes are highly recommended since they provide proper spacing and styrofoam corners. You can also use TV boxes from U-Haul, Lowe's, or Home Depot, but make sure to use packaging materials that provide proper shock absorption protection to the art during shipping. Bubble wrap, packing peanuts, and styrofoam are all good options. Make sure the art itself cannot jostle around in the box.
  • When packaging, never place any packing materials directly on the canvas itself as you run the risk of damage to the art due to the added weight that can cause the canvas to sag onto its stretcher bars on the back.
  • Before shipping, take pictures and videos of the art in the packaging as proof of proper packaging. This will help if you need to file a shipping insurance claim if the art gets damaged during shipping. Insurance claims will not go through if you improperly package your art beforehand, so please take the necessary added precautions to ensure you have done this properly. The admin and moderators can provide advice if anyone needs help on packaging.
  • If the art arrives damaged due to shipping carrier mishandling, do not panic. The receiver or buyer of the art will need to take pictures of the packaging and where the damage on the art has occurred. Do not throw away any of the packaging or ship the damaged art anywhere during the insurance claim process. The insurance claim may ask for the buyer to get a third-party letter stating the art or frame cannot be repaired. Michael's, Hobby Lobby custom framing, or another art framer may be able to provide this.
  • For expensive pieces, we also recommend requiring a signature upon delivery. This helps prevent issues from inclement weather and reduces the risk of package theft.
