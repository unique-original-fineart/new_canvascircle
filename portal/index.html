How does Following work?

Follow artists, sellers, or saved searches to get a push notification the moment a matching listing is approved — no need to keep checking the catalog. Four ways to add a follow:

  • Type an artist's name in the box below (even if they have nothing listed yet).
  • Tap + Follow artist on any listing's detail page.
  • Tap + Follow this seller on a seller's public page.
  • In the catalog, apply filters or a search term and tap 🔔 Save this search — great for catching any new listing that matches a price range, category, Verified-only, Established-only, or other combinations.

Artist follows can be narrowed to Unique/Original or Limited Edition. Saved searches carry whatever filter combo you set up in the catalog.

What counts as fine art on CanvasCircle?

A listing must be one of:

  • Original artwork hand-created by the artist (painting, drawing, sculpture, etc.).
  • Limited edition prints with a numbered edition (e.g. #34/150, AP, HC) and hand-signed by the artist.

Not allowed: open-edition prints (no edition number), posters / reproductions, mass-market home decor, AI-generated art, NFTs.
