How to look up the rate
  1. Click Create a Single Label at the top, then enter the seller's ship-from ZIP in the Ship From field and your address in the Ship To field.
  2. Paste the values above into Type of Packaging → Box or Rigid Packaging and the dimensions + weight fields.
  3. Click the checkbox for Insurance and put in the Declared Package Value ($) as the amount you'll pay for the artwork total.
  4. If you want signature confirmation, click Extra Services and then Signature Confirmation.
  5. Scroll down and click Get Rates and compare UPS and USPS for the best shipping rate option available.
ℹ A note on Pirate Ship's address fields. Pirate Ship requires a full address (name + street) to generate a rate, even when you're just comparing prices. The ZIP code is what actually drives the rate. For the Ship From entry, use any placeholder name and street (e.g. "Seller, 1 Main St") with the seller's ZIP above; the city + state will auto-populate. For Ship To, use your real delivery address. You're only pulling a rate quote, you're not buying a label yet.
Insurance. Add it during checkout on Pirate Ship. Roughly $1 per $100 of declared value. Recommended for every shipment. Pirate Ship's built-in insurance covers up to $10,000 per package.
⚠ This piece exceeds the $10,000 Pirate Ship insurance limit. For full coverage at higher values, options include UPS or FedEx declared-value coverage purchased directly through the carrier, a specialty fine-art shipper (Crozier, Atelier 4, FedEx Custom Critical Art Transport), or a standalone fine-art insurance rider. Message the seller to discuss the shipping path before paying.
Signature confirmation. A flat fee added at checkout. Currently $3.95 via USPS and $6.25 via UPS. Strongly recommended for any piece over $500. Reduces weather and theft risk.
